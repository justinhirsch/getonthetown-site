This isn’t a boosted post

It reaches a different person entirely.

  • Your followers already know you. A post on your own page reaches the people who decided to follow you. The person worth winning is the one who typed “things to do in Charleston” and has never heard your name.
  • You appear where people are choosing. Your event sits on the list for that night and as a pin on the map, next to everything else on at the same hour — not in a feed between a birthday and an ad.
  • The link goes straight to you. Tickets, your site, your phone number, whatever you give us. We don’t sit between you and the sale, and we take no commission on a link you give us.
  • It stays up until the doors open. A post is gone in a few hours. A listing is there every time somebody checks what’s on this week.

How it works
