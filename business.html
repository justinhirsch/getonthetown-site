OnTheTown shows people everything going on in their city this week and this weekend. Getting your events on it takes about two minutes.

How it works

No account, no contract, nothing to install.

  • 1Send us the event below, and a photo if you have one. The photo is the part that matters — listings with a real picture get looked at far more than ones without.
  • 2We check the details and publish it. Usually same day.
  • 3It appears for anyone browsing that city, on the list and on the map, with a link straight to your tickets or page.
Why we ask you for the photo. We don't take pictures off venue websites — we don't have the rights to them. If you send one, we can use it. That's the only way your event shows up with your artwork instead of a plain tile.
